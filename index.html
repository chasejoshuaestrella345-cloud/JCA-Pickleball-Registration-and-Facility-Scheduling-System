Play fair, play safe

Before you hit the court

A few simple guidelines help everyone enjoy their time at JCA Sports.

⏰

Arrive on time

Please arrive a few minutes early. Your reserved session lasts one hour.

👟

Wear proper gear

Use suitable indoor sportswear and non-marking court shoes.

🤝

Respect the space

Take care of the equipment and leave the facility clean for the next group.

📣

Cancel if plans change

Let the facility team know if you can no longer make your reservation.
